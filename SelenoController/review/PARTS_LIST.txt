SELENO CONTROLLER - PCB PARTS LIST (v0.8)
=========================================
Checked line by line against SELENO.kicad_pcb (the authority) on 2026-10-15,
after the design-check changes (C47 added, order-list values applied) and the
power change (two AP62300 bucks -> TPS5430 buck + AMS1117 LDO).
The board, the schematic and this list agree (DRC schematic parity 0).

v0.8 change (vs v0.7): the USB hub, crystal, CH340C, USB-A socket, USB ESD chips, camera
PTC and their 22 passives are gone. The Pi Zero 2 now connects through J7, a
1x10 2.54 mm header wired to the EVEN row of the Pi header (pins 2-20); see
section 9 for the cable. The camera plugs into the Pi's own USB port.

Power change (2026-10-15): the two AP62300WU bucks (an extended part, ~$3
fee) are gone. U6 is now a TPS5430DDAR 12 V -> 5 V buck and U9 an AMS1117-3.3
LDO making 3.3 V from the 5 V rail, both JLC basic. See POWER CHANGE below.

Board contents (122 footprints):
  - 68 parts in the KiCad BOM (33 lines in the BOM export): sections 1-6.
  - 18 parts excluded from the KiCad BOM that still have to be bought
    (AliExpress / hand-solder): C1, all connectors (J1, J2, J3, J7, P1, S1,
    SW1-SW7) and the R25-R28 sense resistors (sections 7 and 9).
  - H1-H4 (M2.5 mounting holes) and 32 test pads (TP1-TP31, TP33; TP32 left
    with the BUCK_EN divider) are board-only copper: nothing to buy.

Sourcing plan:
  - JLC basic / promotional extended parts (no loading fee).
  - All QFN chips from JLC, even if extended ($3 loading fee per part type).
  - ESP32 (and maybe a few others) from personal supplies.
  - Everything else from AliExpress.

The C-numbers in sections 1 and 2 come from Alex's JLC order list (not
re-verified here). Elsewhere the "Source" column is still a first guess;
"Check" = needs a lookup. The symbols' "LCSC Part #" fields are still empty.

  C53      100pF 50V 0402 (compensation, VSENSE-GND)      C1546 basic
  C54      100nF 50V 0402 (compensation, with R39)        C307331
  R39      330R 0402 (compensation, with C54)             C25104
  (U6, U9 and L2 keep their references; their old parts are counted as removed.)
Changed values:
  C36      100nF 50V -> 10nF 50V (TPS5430 boot cap must be 0.01uF)
  R29      27k 1% -> 15k 1% ; R30 5.1k 1% -> 4.7k 1%  (5.12 V with the 1.221 V ref)
  L2       3.3uH Wurth -> 15uH FXL0840-150-M (new footprint)
Kept as they were: C35 10uF 50V (U6 input), C37, C38 (5 V output), C15, C16 (moved
next to U9).
U6 EN is left open (internal pull-up, 5.3 V UVLO): TI says never tie it to GND
through a resistor, so the BUCK_EN divider is gone.
The compensation (C52, C53, C54, R39) follows TI datasheet Fig 7-11 for
all-ceramic output caps. A loop model built from the datasheet gives
57-72 deg phase margin for 35-60 uF effective output capacitance and
0.2-2 A of load.
