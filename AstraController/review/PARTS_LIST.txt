WAYS TO REDUCE PART TYPES
-------------------------
- 100nF: three versions (no voltage, 25V, 50V), 21 total. One 50V 0402 part
  can cover all of them.
- 10k: plain 10k and 10k 1% can be one part (JLC basic resistors are already
  1%). 11 total.
- 10uF 0805: C15, C25, C33 have no voltage rating. Check which rail each sits
  on before picking a part.


LOADING FEE ESTIMATE
--------------------
QFN parts (TMC2209, MCP23017, USB2512B) + Y1 crystal (C70590): 4 types x $3 = ~$12.
CH340C and AP62300WU are likely extended too: +~$6 unless JLC has them as
promotional extended parts or they're sourced elsewhere.
