Advanced settings
3D Planetarium Controls

  • Drag mouse to rotate the sky
  • Scroll wheel to zoom in/out (auto-adjusts magnitude to show fainter stars when zoomed in)
  • Click a star/planet for info
  • Right-click magnitude slider for custom values
  • Touch: swipe with one finger to rotate the sky
  • Touch: pinch with two fingers to zoom in/out
  • Touch: tap a star/planet for info
  • Touch: use the ☰ button to show/hide these controls
  • Touch: press and hold the magnitude slider for custom values
  • Adjust magnitude filter to show/hide faint stars
  • Toggle "Auto-adjust magnitude on zoom" to link zoom level with star visibility
  • Set your latitude/longitude for local sky orientation
  • Toggle stars/planets visibility
  • Toggle horizon grid (altitude/azimuth with N/E/S/W)
  • Toggle equatorial grid (HA/DEC: Hour Angle and Declination)
  • Toggle the ecliptic line
  • Toggle constellation figures for the 24 most prominent constellations, with optional names. The line data comes from d3-celestial and every vertex has been snapped onto a star in this catalogue, so the figures meet the stars they are drawn from
  • Toggle star names to label named objects - 407 stars carrying an official IAU proper name, plus the named clusters, nebulae and galaxies in this catalogue. Search accepts those names too (try "Mizar", "Algol" or "Andromeda Galaxy")
  • Tick "Unknown magnitude" to show catalogue objects that have no recorded V-Mag (drawn in blue); they are excluded from the magnitude slider because there is no honest value to compare against
  • Tick "Render stats" for a readout of how many objects the last frame drew and how long it took
  • Reset view to default orientation and zoom
  • Use the Time control to preview the sky at any date/time; the grid labels show Hour Angle (HA).
  • Tip: Focus the Time box and press Arrow Up/Down to adjust with rollover. Move the caret over year/month/day/hour/minute to change that unit. If caret isn't available, use Shift (hour), Ctrl (day), Alt (month), or Ctrl+Shift (year).
